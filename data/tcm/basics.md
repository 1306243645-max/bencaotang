# 中医基础理论 (TCM Fundamentals)

## 核心概念

### 阴阳 (Yin-Yang)
- **Yin**: cold, rest, passive, interior, deficiency, chronic
- **Yang**: heat, activity, active, exterior, excess, acute
- Health = Yin-Yang balance; disease = imbalance

### 五行 (Five Elements / Five Phases)
| Element | Organ (Yin) | Organ (Yang) | Emotion | Season | Taste |
|---------|-------------|--------------|----------|--------|-------|
| Wood | Liver | Gallbladder | Anger | Spring | Sour |
| Fire | Heart | Small Intestine | Joy | Summer | Bitter |
| Earth | Spleen | Stomach | Worry | Late Summer | Sweet |
| Metal | Lung | Large Intestine | Grief | Autumn | Pungent |
| Water | Kidney | Bladder | Fear | Winter | Salty |

### 气血津液 (Qi, Blood, Body Fluids)
- **Qi**: vital energy, drives all physiological functions
- **Blood**: nourishes and moistens the body
- **Body Fluids (Jin-Ye)**: all physiological fluids

## 病因 (Causes of Disease)

### 六淫 (Six External Pathogenic Factors)
1. Wind (风) - sudden onset, moving symptoms, affects upper body
2. Cold (寒) - contraction, stagnation, pain
3. Heat/Fire (热/火) - fever, inflammation, red face
4. Dampness (湿) - heaviness, stickiness, chronic
5. Dryness (燥) - dry skin, throat, cough
6. Summer-Heat (暑) - only in summer, dehydration

### 七情 (Seven Emotions)
Anger → Liver | Joy → Heart | Worry → Spleen | Grief → Lung | Fear → Kidney | Fright → Heart | Sadness → Lung

## 八纲辨证 (Eight-Principle Pattern Differentiation)

1. **Exterior vs Interior** (表里) - location of disease
2. **Cold vs Heat** (寒热) - nature of disease
3. **Deficiency vs Excess** (虚实) - strength of pathogenic factors vs body resistance
4. **Yin vs Yang** (阴阳) - overarching category

## Common Patterns

### Qi Deficiency
- Fatigue, weak voice, spontaneous sweating, pale tongue, weak pulse
- Diet: warm, easy-to-digest foods. Avoid raw/cold foods.

### Blood Deficiency
- Pale complexion, dizziness, dry skin, scanty periods, pale tongue
- Diet: red dates, goji berries, black sesame, liver

### Yin Deficiency
- Dry mouth, night sweats, hot palms/soles, red tongue with little coating
- Diet: cooling, moistening foods. Avoid spicy/hot foods.

### Yang Deficiency
- Cold limbs, aversion to cold, frequent urination, pale puffy tongue
- Diet: warming foods (ginger, cinnamon, lamb). Avoid cold foods.

### Liver Qi Stagnation
- Distension, mood swings, sighing, PMS, wiry pulse
- Diet: green leafy vegetables, peppermint tea. Avoid alcohol.

### Damp-Heat
- Heaviness, sticky taste, yellow greasy tongue coating, loose stool
- Diet: bitter foods, barley, mung beans. Avoid greasy/sweet foods.

## 四诊 (Four Diagnostic Methods)

1. **Inspection** (望) - observe complexion, tongue, posture
2. **Listening/Smelling** (闻) - voice quality, breath odor
3. **Inquiry** (问) - ask about symptoms, lifestyle, diet, emotions
4. **Palpation** (切) - pulse diagnosis (described, not performed remotely)
