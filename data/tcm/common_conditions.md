# 常见症状 TCM 辨证 (Common Conditions & Pattern Differentiation)

## Insomnia (失眠)

### Heart Blood Deficiency
- Difficulty falling asleep, waking easily, palpitations, pale tongue
- **Diet**: longan, red dates, lotus seed, warm milk before bed
- **Lifestyle**: avoid screens 1hr before bed, warm foot bath

### Liver Fire Flaring
- Irritability, dream-disturbed sleep, bitter taste, red tongue
- **Diet**: chrysanthemum tea, celery juice, avoid alcohol
- **Lifestyle**: evening wind-down routine, meditation

### Yin Deficiency with Heat
- Night waking, night sweats, dry mouth, hot palms/soles
- **Diet**: lily bulb, black fungus, avoid spicy food
- **Lifestyle**: avoid late-night exercise, cool bedroom

---

## Digestive Issues (脾胃不适)

### Spleen Qi Deficiency
- Bloating after meals, loose stool, fatigue, poor appetite
- **Diet**: congee, cooked vegetables, ginger tea, small frequent meals
- **Avoid**: raw foods, cold drinks, dairy

### Food Stagnation
- Fullness, acid reflux, foul belching, thick greasy tongue coating
- **Diet**: radish, hawthorn, light meals, fasting for one meal
- **Lifestyle**: walk after meals, don't eat late

### Damp-Heat in Spleen-Stomach
- Nausea, sticky taste, yellow tongue coating, loose urgent stool
- **Diet**: barley water, mung bean soup, avoid greasy/spicy
- **Lifestyle**: avoid humid environments

---

## Stress & Anxiety (情志问题)

### Liver Qi Stagnation
- Irritability, mood swings, chest/rib-side distension, sighing
- **Diet**: peppermint tea, rose tea, green leafy vegetables
- **Acupressure**: LV3 (Tai Chong), LI4 (He Gu)
- **Exercise**: walking, yoga, tai chi

### Heart-Spleen Deficiency
- Overthinking, worry, poor memory, fatigue
- **Diet**: red dates, longan, lotus seed porridge
- **Lifestyle**: regular sleep schedule, mindfulness

### Kidney Deficiency with Anxiety
- Fear, low back weakness, frequent urination
- **Diet**: walnuts, black sesame, goji berries
- **Lifestyle**: adequate rest, reduce overwork

---

## Fatigue (疲劳)

### Qi Deficiency
- Constant tiredness, weak voice, spontaneous sweating
- **Diet**: astragalus tea, chicken soup with ginger
- **Avoid**: overwork, excessive exercise

### Dampness Obstructing
- Heavy limbs, brain fog, sluggishness
- **Diet**: barley, coix seed, avoid dairy and sugar
- **Exercise**: sweating helps move dampness

### Kidney Yang Deficiency
- Deep fatigue, cold limbs, low back pain, low libido
- **Diet**: lamb, walnuts, ginger, cinnamon
- **Lifestyle**: keep warm, adequate sleep

---

## Headaches (头痛)

### Liver Yang Rising
- Throbbing pain, temples, irritability, red face
- **Diet**: chrysanthemum tea, celery
- **Acupressure**: GB20 (Feng Chi), Tai Yang point
- **Avoid**: alcohol, late nights

### Blood Deficiency Headache
- Dull ache, worse with fatigue, better with rest
- **Diet**: red dates, goji, black chicken soup

### Wind-Cold Headache
- Occipital pain, stiff neck, aversion to cold
- **Diet**: ginger and scallion tea, keep warm
- **Therapy**: warm compress on neck

---

## Women's Health (妇科)

### Pre-Menstrual Syndrome (PMS)
- Often Liver Qi Stagnation: sore breasts, mood swings
- **Diet**: limit salt, caffeine; peppermint tea
- **Acupressure**: LV3, LI4

### Menopausal Syndrome
- Often Yin Deficiency: hot flashes, night sweats, dry skin
- **Diet**: soy products, black sesame, lily bulb
- **Avoid**: spicy food, alcohol, coffee
