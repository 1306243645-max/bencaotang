## AUSTRALIAN REGULATORY NOTE

In Australia, Chinese herbs are regulated by the TGA (Therapeutic Goods Administration).
Some herbs are **prohibited or restricted**:
- **Aristocholic acid-containing** herbs (Aristolochia, Asarum species — some) — BANNED
- **Ephedra (Ma Huang)** — strictly regulated (Schedule 4)
- **Aconite (Fu Zi, Chuan Wu)** — must be processed; restricted
- **He Shou Wu (Polygonum multiflorum)** — liver toxicity warning, TGA advisory

**Always check TGA's ARTG (Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods) before importing/supplying any herbs.**
