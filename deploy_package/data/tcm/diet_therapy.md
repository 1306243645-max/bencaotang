# 中医食疗 (TCM Dietary Therapy)

## Food Energetics

### Thermal Nature
- **Hot**: chilli, black pepper, ginger (dried), cinnamon bark
- **Warm**: ginger (fresh), garlic, chicken, lamb, brown sugar, oats
- **Neutral**: rice, corn, carrot, egg, milk, honey, goji berry
- **Cool**: celery, cucumber, pear, green tea, tofu, wheat
- **Cold**: watermelon, banana, mung bean, seaweed, bitter gourd

### Five Flavors & Organ Affinity
| Flavor | Organ | Action | Examples |
|--------|-------|--------|----------|
| Sour | Liver | Astringent | Lemon, vinegar, plum |
| Bitter | Heart | Drying, clearing heat | Bitter gourd, dandelion |
| Sweet | Spleen | Tonifying, harmonizing | Rice, dates, honey |
| Pungent | Lung | Dispersing, moving qi | Ginger, garlic, mint |
| Salty | Kidney | Softening, descending | Seaweed, miso |

## Pattern-Specific Dietary Advice

### For Qi Deficiency
- **Recommended**: rice, oats, sweet potato, chicken, beef, dates, astragalus root
- **Avoid**: raw vegetables, cold drinks, excessive salads

### For Blood Deficiency
- **Recommended**: red dates, goji berries, dark leafy greens, liver, black sesame, longan
- **Avoid**: excessive spicy food, alcohol, caffeine

### For Yin Deficiency
- **Recommended**: pear, lily bulb, lotus seed, black fungus, duck, tofu, honey
- **Avoid**: spicy food, late-night eating, alcohol, smoking

### For Damp-Heat
- **Recommended**: barley, mung bean, bitter gourd, celery, lotus leaf tea
- **Avoid**: greasy food, dairy, sugar, alcohol, fried food

### For Liver Qi Stagnation
- **Recommended**: green leafy vegetables, peppermint tea, rose tea, citrus peel
- **Avoid**: alcohol, excessive spices, heavy/fatty meals

### For Phlegm-Damp
- **Recommended**: radish, seaweed, pear, coix seed (Job's tears), tangerine peel
- **Avoid**: dairy, sweets, greasy food, cold drinks

## Australian Seasonal Eating Guide

### Spring (Sep-Nov)
- Focus: Liver health, gentle detox
- Foods: green vegetables, mint, lemon, sprouts
- Activity: morning walks, stretching

### Summer (Dec-Feb)
- Focus: Heart health, cooling
- Foods: watermelon, cucumber, mung beans, chrysanthemum tea
- Activity: swimming, moderate exercise

### Autumn (Mar-May)
- Focus: Lung health, moistening
- Foods: pear, white fungus, lotus seed, honey
- Activity: breathing exercises

### Winter (Jun-Aug)
- Focus: Kidney health, warming
- Foods: lamb, ginger, cinnamon, black beans, walnuts
- Activity: gentle indoor exercise, adequate rest
