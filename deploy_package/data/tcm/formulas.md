# 方剂学基础 (Classic Chinese Herbal Formulas Reference)

> ⚠️ Educational reference only. Formulas must be prescribed and dosed by a registered TCM practitioner.
> Individual herbs, dosages, and combinations vary based on exact pattern differentiation.

---

## 解表剂 (Exterior-Releasing Formulas)

### Ma Huang Tang (麻黄汤) — Ephedra Decoction
- **Pattern**: Wind-Cold exterior excess
- **Symptoms**: Aversion to cold, fever without sweating, headache, body aches, panting
- **Composition**: Ma Huang, Gui Zhi, Xing Ren, Gan Cao
- ⚠️ Not used in Australia (Ephedra restrictions)

### Gui Zhi Tang (桂枝汤) — Cinnamon Twig Decoction
- **Pattern**: Wind-Cold exterior deficiency (tai yang disorder)
- **Symptoms**: Aversion to wind, mild fever with sweating, nasal congestion, floating pulse
- **Composition**: Gui Zhi, Bai Shao, Sheng Jiang, Da Zao, Gan Cao
- **Note**: One of the most important formulas in TCM — harmonizes ying (nutritive) and wei (defensive) qi

### Yin Qiao San (银翘散) — Honeysuckle & Forsythia Powder
- **Pattern**: Wind-Heat attacking the exterior
- **Symptoms**: Fever, slight aversion to cold, sore throat, thirst, cough
- **Composition**: Jin Yin Hua, Lian Qiao, Bo He, Niu Bang Zi, Jie Geng, Dan Dou Chi, Jing Jie, Dan Zhu Ye, Gan Cao, Lu Gen
- **Note**: Very commonly used pattern in clinic

## 清热剂 (Heat-Clearing Formulas)

### Bai Hu Tang (白虎汤) — White Tiger Decoction
- **Pattern**: Yang Ming channel heat (Qi-level heat)
- **Symptoms**: High fever, profuse sweating, intense thirst, flooding rapid pulse
- **Composition**: Shi Gao, Zhi Mu, Gan Cao, Jing Mi

### Long Dan Xie Gan Tang (龙胆泻肝汤) — Gentiana Liver-Draining Decoction
- **Pattern**: Liver-gallbladder damp-heat, or liver fire flaring upward
- **Symptoms**: Irritability, bitter taste, rib-side pain, yellow greasy tongue, red eyes, headache
- **Composition**: Long Dan Cao, Huang Qin, Zhi Zi, Ze Xie, Mu Tong, Che Qian Zi, Dang Gui, Sheng Di Huang, Chai Hu, Gan Cao

### Zhi Bai Di Huang Wan (知柏地黄丸) — Anemarrhena-Phellodendron Rehmannia Pill
- **Pattern**: Kidney yin deficiency with deficiency fire
- **Symptoms**: Night sweats, hot palms/soles, dry mouth at night, nocturnal emissions, low-grade fever
- **Composition**: Zhi Mu, Huang Bai + Liu Wei Di Huang Wan (Shu Di, Shan Zhu Yu, Shan Yao, Ze Xie, Fu Ling, Mu Dan Pi)
- **Note**: Very widely used in clinic for menopausal night sweats, chronic low-grade inflammation

## 补益剂 (Tonic Formulas)

### Si Jun Zi Tang (四君子汤) — Four Gentlemen Decoction
- **Pattern**: Spleen qi deficiency (foundational qi tonic)
- **Symptoms**: Fatigue, poor appetite, loose stool, pale tongue, weak pulse
- **Composition**: Ren Shen (or Dang Shen), Bai Zhu, Fu Ling, Gan Cao
- **Note**: The foundation formula for Spleen qi tonification — many formulas derive from this

### Si Wu Tang (四物汤) — Four Substances Decoction
- **Pattern**: Blood deficiency and/or blood stasis
- **Symptoms**: Pale complexion, dizziness, scanty/irregular menses, pale tongue, thin pulse
- **Composition**: Dang Gui, Shu Di Huang, Bai Shao, Chuan Xiong
- **Note**: The foundational blood tonic formula

### Ba Zhen Tang (八珍汤) — Eight Treasure Decoction
- **Pattern**: Qi and blood dual deficiency
- **Symptoms**: Fatigue + pale complexion + poor appetite + palpitations
- **Composition**: Si Jun Zi Tang + Si Wu Tang = Qi + Blood tonic combined

### Liu Wei Di Huang Wan (六味地黄丸) — Six-Ingredient Rehmannia Pill
- **Pattern**: Kidney yin deficiency
- **Symptoms**: Sore lower back, tinnitus, night sweats, dry mouth, nocturnal emissions
- **Composition**: Shu Di Huang, Shan Zhu Yu, Shan Yao, Ze Xie, Fu Ling, Mu Dan Pi
- **Note**: The "Three Tonics + Three Drainers" design — classic formula architecture
- **Australian name**: Often sold as "Rehmannia Six" or "Six Flavor Teapills"

### Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan (金匮肾气丸) — Kidney Qi Pill from the Golden Cabinet
- **Pattern**: Kidney yang deficiency
- **Symptoms**: Cold limbs, frequent urination, lower back weakness, edema, pale puffy tongue
- **Composition**: Liu Wei Di Huang Wan + Fu Zi (Aconite) + Gui Zhi
- ⚠️ Contains processed aconite — restricted, requires TCM practitioner prescription

### Gui Pi Tang (归脾汤) — Restore the Spleen Decoction
- **Pattern**: Heart-Spleen deficiency (overthinking/anxiety pattern)
- **Symptoms**: Insomnia with worry, palpitations, poor memory, fatigue, pale tongue
- **Composition**: Ren Shen, Huang Qi, Bai Zhu, Fu Shen, Dang Gui, Long Yan Rou, Suan Zao Ren, Yuan Zhi, Mu Xiang, Gan Cao
- **Note**: Excellent formula for "overthinking insomnia"

### Sheng Mai San (生脉散) — Generate the Pulse Powder
- **Pattern**: Qi and yin deficiency (post-illness, summer heat, or chronic fatigue)
- **Symptoms**: Fatigue, shortness of breath, spontaneous sweating, dry mouth
- **Composition**: Ren Shen, Mai Dong, Wu Wei Zi
- **Note**: Very gentle, only 3 herbs — often used for recovery

## 理气剂 (Qi-Regulating Formulas)

### Xiao Yao San (逍遥散) — Free & Easy Wanderer Powder
- **Pattern**: Liver qi stagnation with spleen deficiency (with or without blood deficiency)
- **Symptoms**: Mood swings, PMS, rib-side distension, sighing, fatigue, poor appetite
- **Composition**: Chai Hu, Dang Gui, Bai Shao, Bai Zhu, Fu Ling, Sheng Jiang, Bo He, Gan Cao
- **Note**: 🔥 One of the most prescribed TCM formulas — classic stress/PMS pattern
- **Variant**: Jia Wei Xiao Yao San (加味逍遥散) — adds Mu Dan Pi + Zhi Zi for liver fire with heat

### Ban Xia Hou Po Tang (半夏厚朴汤) — Pinellia-Magnolia Bark Decoction
- **Pattern**: Phlegm-qi stagnation in the throat ("plum pit qi" — globus sensation)
- **Symptoms**: Sensation of lump in throat, emotional distress, chest tightness, nausea
- **Composition**: Ban Xia, Hou Po, Fu Ling, Zi Su Ye, Sheng Jiang

### Chai Hu Shu Gan San (柴胡疏肝散) — Bupleurum Liver-Soothing Powder
- **Pattern**: Liver qi stagnation with pain
- **Symptoms**: Rib-side + chest pain/distension, belching, mood swings
- **Composition**: Xiao Yao San base modified — Chai Hu, Bai Shao, Zhi Ke, Chuan Xiong, Xiang Fu, Chen Pi, Gan Cao

## 祛湿剂 (Dampness-Resolving Formulas)

### Ping Wei San (平胃散) — Calm the Stomach Powder
- **Pattern**: Dampness obstructing the spleen-stomach
- **Symptoms**: Abdominal fullness, nausea, loose stool, thick greasy tongue coating
- **Composition**: Cang Zhu, Hou Po, Chen Pi, Gan Cao

### Huo Xiang Zheng Qi San (藿香正气散) — Agastache Qi-Correcting Powder
- **Pattern**: External wind-cold + internal dampness stagnation
- **Symptoms**: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, headache, abdominal pain — "summer flu" pattern
- **Composition**: Huo Xiang, Hou Po, Chen Pi, Fu Ling, Bai Zhu, Ban Xia, Da Fu Pi, Zi Su Ye, Jie Geng, Gan Cao, Sheng Jiang, Da Zao
- **Note**: Widely available over the counter in Australia as "Huo Xiang Zheng Qi Wan"

## 安神剂 (Shen-Calming Formulas)

### Suan Zao Ren Tang (酸枣仁汤) — Zizyphus Seed Decoction
- **Pattern**: Liver blood deficiency with heat disturbing the heart shen
- **Symptoms**: Insomnia, irritability, night waking, palpitations
- **Composition**: Suan Zao Ren, Chuan Xiong, Zhi Mu, Fu Ling, Gan Cao
- **Note**: One of the classic insomnia formulas — nourishes liver blood while clearing deficiency heat

### Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan (天王补心丹) — Heavenly Emperor's Heart-Tonifying Pill
- **Pattern**: Heart-kidney yin deficiency with disturbed shen
- **Symptoms**: Insomnia, palpitations, anxiety, night sweats, poor memory, mouth/tongue ulcers
- **Composition**: Sheng Di Huang, Xuan Shen, Mai Dong, Tian Men Dong, Dang Gui, Dan Shen, Ren Shen, Fu Ling, Yuan Zhi, Suan Zao Ren, Bai Zi Ren, Wu Wei Zi, Jie Geng
- **Note**: A comprehensive formula for chronic anxiety-insomnia with yin deficiency

### Gan Mai Da Zao Tang (甘麦大枣汤) — Licorice-Wheat-Jujube Decoction
- **Pattern**: Heart qi deficiency with shen disturbance ("visceral agitation / 脏躁")
- **Symptoms**: Emotional lability, spontaneous crying, restlessness, frequent sighing, yawning
- **Composition**: Gan Cao, Fu Xiao Mai, Da Zao
- **Note**: Only 3 herbs — very gentle, food-grade, can be used as a tea

## 消食剂 (Food-Stagnation Formulas)

### Bao He Wan (保和丸) — Harmony-Preserving Pill
- **Pattern**: Food stagnation in the stomach
- **Symptoms**: Fullness, acid reflux, foul belching, thick greasy tongue coating, nausea
- **Composition**: Shan Zha, Shen Qu, Lai Fu Zi, Chen Pi, Ban Xia, Fu Ling, Lian Qiao

---

## 活血化瘀剂 (Blood-Stasis Formulas)

### Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang (血府逐瘀汤) — Blood Mansion Stasis-Expelling Decoction
- **Pattern**: Blood stasis in the chest
- **Symptoms**: Chest pain/prickling sensation, palpitations, insomnia, dark purple tongue, choppy pulse
- **Composition**: Tao Ren, Hong Hua, Dang Gui, Sheng Di Huang, Chuan Xiong, Chi Shao, Niu Xi, Jie Geng, Chai Hu, Zhi Ke, Gan Cao

### TGA Regulation:
- Listed medicines (AUST L) — lower risk, pre-approved ingredients
- Registered medicines (AUST R) — higher risk, full efficacy/safety review
- Practitioners must source herbs from TGA-compliant suppliers
- Certain herbs restricted or banned (Aristolochia species, etc.)

⚠️ **Formulas should ONLY be prescribed by a qualified practitioner** after proper diagnosis including tongue and pulse examination.
